What we offer:

Orbital 360 caters for a wide variety of events, venues, functions, parties and more.

Our standard pricing rate is R850 per hour for rental events. For business marketing where your business name is displayed on the template, the rental is free, however customers will be charged R50 - R100 per photo depending on the venue as well as extras. You will be able to choose your own template as well as your own song - mainly for event functions. After you have taken your photo, you will receive your own personalised barcode containing your photos.
